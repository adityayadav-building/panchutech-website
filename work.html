What The Photos Don't Show

The calculation behind the finish.

A finished auditorium looks like interior work. The part that decides whether it performs happens earlier — room modelling, reverberation targets, throw-distance calculation, sightline verification row by row.

By the time drapery and seating go in, the acoustic and optical decisions are already locked. That's the difference between a room that looks like a cinema and a room that behaves like one.
