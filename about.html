About PanchuTech

Based in New Delhi. Built for full-scope cinema engineering.

PanchuTech Cine Systems Pvt. Ltd. (PTCS) is a cinema technology and home entertainment engineering company. Where a typical AV vendor sells equipment, we specify and build complete systems as one engineered whole.

Who We Are

Seating, acoustics, projection and home theater — engineered as one system.

PanchuTech covers every discipline a screening room needs beyond audio — seating, acoustic treatment, projection, and full home theater builds — with the same engineering discipline across all of them.

We work in close collaboration with Cine Aryan, our audio and cinema sound specialist partner. On turnkey projects, PanchuTech leads the full build while Cine Aryan engineers the audio system within it — so nothing is handled by a generalist.

That split exists because cinema sound is its own deep discipline, and so is everything around it. Rather than have one team stretch across every skill at once, PanchuTech and Cine Aryan each stay specialists in their own scope — and coordinate as one delivery team on shared projects.
