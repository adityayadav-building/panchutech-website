How a Build Runs

From site survey to final calibration.

Five stages, run in sequence, with every discipline scheduled against the same timeline rather than layered on afterward.

01 — Site Survey

Room geometry, acoustic and structural assessment on location — dimensions, materials, existing services and access constraints, all recorded before design starts.

02 — System Design

Audio, projection, acoustic and seating specification engineered together, so decisions in one discipline account for their effect on the others.

03 — Supply & Fitout

Equipment sourcing and installation across every discipline, sequenced against the wider construction or fit-out schedule.

04 — Calibration

On-site tuning and measurement in the finished space — acoustic response, projector alignment and audio levels are set to the room as-built, not to a spec sheet.

05 — Handover

Documented settings, sign-off against the original brief, and a point of contact for ongoing support after commissioning.
