The Differentiator

Specialists first. Everything else follows from that.

We don't sell equipment off a shelf. Every install starts with calculation — room geometry, throw ratios, reverberation time, load paths — before a single product is chosen. Design, supply and installation sit under one accountable team, so nothing falls into the gap between vendors.

In Collaboration With Cine Aryan

One turnkey build, two specialists.

PanchuTech leads the full build — seating, acoustics, projection and home theater. Our audio partner, Cine Aryan, engineers the sound system within it. On a turnkey project, that means the acoustic modelling, the seating rake and the projection throw are all specified by PanchuTech, while the loudspeaker system, amplification and calibration are handled by Cine Aryan — inside the same schedule, under the same sign-off. One accountable delivery, no generalist gaps.
