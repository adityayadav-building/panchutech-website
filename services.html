Common Questions

Before you get in touch.

A few things clients usually ask when scoping a project.

Can I hire PanchuTech for a single discipline, like just seating or acoustics?

Yes. Each of the six disciplines can be engaged on its own — you don't need a full turnkey build to work with us on a single scope like seating, projection or acoustic treatment.

Do you supply the audio system directly?

Audio and cinema sound engineering is delivered through our partner, Cine Aryan, working inside PanchuTech's build schedule. On a turnkey project you deal with one team; the audio discipline itself is led by Cine Aryan.

What's the difference between a home theater and a turnkey cinema build for you?

The engineering process is the same — survey, model, specify, install, calibrate. The scale and the seating/acoustic requirements differ, but a residential 5.1/7.1 room and a commercial screen both go through the same design discipline.

Do you work outside New Delhi?

We're based in New Delhi and site surveys are available across NCR and pan-India for commercial builds — get in touch with your location and we'll confirm feasibility.

Not sure which discipline you need?

Most projects start with a site survey. We'll tell you exactly what the room needs.
